DATA / 01

First, the uncomfortable truth

Free data can teach you the mechanism. It cannot pretend to be a colocated feed.

A delayed quote, an end-of-day chain, an aggregated L2 book, and order-by-order L3 are different instruments. A serious test names the difference instead of hiding it.

A practical bridge between a research question and a reproducible experiment. QuantPad presents a workflow where an agent can inspect available coverage, retrieve market data, and produce analysis-ready artifacts instead of leaving a retail researcher to stitch every tool together alone.

Normalized historical and live schemas across supported venues. Depending on venue, you may find trades, MBP quotes, or MBO events. Promotional credits change, so verify current terms before planning a dataset around them.

A realistic build order

Earn complexity.
Do not buy it first.

01 / LEARN

Daily bars and contract specs

Reproduce a simple hypothesis with transparent data. Add correct expiry, roll, multiplier, tick value, and session calendars.

02 / REPLAY

Record a public L2 stream

Build a collector with timestamps, sequence checks, reconnect logic, and raw message storage. Your dataset becomes yours.

03 / EXECUTE

Add bid/ask and costs

Model spread, fees, slippage, latency, partial fills, and rejected orders before trusting a result.

04 / SCALE

Pay only for the missing field

When a question genuinely needs historical MBO or complete option quotes, buy the smallest licensed slice that answers it.

DATA DISCIPLINE

Never download a “free” dataset without checking timestamp timezone, corporate actions, contract rolls, survivorship, exchange rights, and whether quotes are executable.
